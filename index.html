Introduction to New Oxford Public School

Situated at Baandh Road, Chondi, Barh (Patna, Bihar), New Oxford Public School is an esteemed educational institution dedicated to providing balanced, modern, and value-based education for children from Pre-Nursery (PNC) through Class 8.

Following the rigorous standards of the CBSE Curriculum Pattern, our school integrates foundational literacy, numeracy, analytical reasoning, and creative expression. We believe every child is endowed with unique potential, and our structured classrooms nurture intellect, discipline, and emotional strength.

📚

CBSE Pattern Academic Rigor

Curriculum designed to develop critical inquiry, scientific temperament, and language mastery from early childhood.

🌐

Multi-Lingual Heritage (Urdu & Sanskrit)

Linguistic Heritage & Moral Values

Special Study in Urdu & Sanskrit

New Oxford Public School stands apart by providing structured curriculum and dedicated faculty for both Urdu and Sanskrit, fostering respect, classical eloquence, and intellectual enrichment.

اردو

Urdu Language & Literature

فروغِ علم و ادب (Promotion of Knowledge & Literature)

Urdu is taught with methodical care from basic Huruf-e-Tahajji (alphabet) up to rich prose, poetry (Nazm & Ghazal), correct pronunciation (Talaffuz), and moral stories.

✓ Reading & Calligraphy (Khushkhati): Emphasis on beautiful handwriting in Nastaliq script.
✓ Vocabulary & Grammatical Rules (Qawaid): Clear understanding of syntax, idioms, and comprehension.
✓ Moral Stories (Akhlaqiyat): Stories of empathy, honesty, and community brotherhood.
✓ Exam Assessment (Class 1–8): Full inclusion in the report cards and examination grading.
संस्कृतम्

Sanskrit Language & Shlokas

विद्या ददाति विनयं (Knowledge Bestows Humility)

The mother of classical Indian languages, Sanskrit instills scientific grammatical logic, pure phonetics, and ethical wisdom from timeless subhashitas and shlokas.

✓ Shloka Recitation & Pronunciation: Chanting of foundational mantras and ethical couplets.
✓ Dhatu Roop & Shabda Roop: Systematic grasp of Sanskrit verb roots, declensions, and grammar.
✓ Cultural Wisdom & Hitopadesha: Value-rich fables developing character and righteousness.
✓ CBSE Grading Standard: Full evaluation included in class 1 to 8 marksheet portals.
